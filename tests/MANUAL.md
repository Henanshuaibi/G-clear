# Installed-device acceptance checklist

These checks require installation and manual interaction; they are not claimed as passed by the automated suite.

- Fresh install: disclosure appears before system accessibility settings; denied authorization leaves status disabled; enabling reports connected after returning. Restricted-settings and missing settings intents give actionable guidance.
- Default selection: all APPs off, search by Chinese name and package, toggles survive process recreation and reboot. Jump guard requires separate confirmation and is disabled when skip is turned off.
- Fixture positive: choose test APP, launch from home, automatic callback and exactly one skip increment; wait without more increments. Master pause prevents actions. Negative fixture never clicks tutorial or lower-screen skip.
- Real APP: record device model, Android version and exact APP version; observe standard skip, unreadable/self-drawn button, and mismatched position. Confirm false-positive escape by global pause or per-APP switch.
- Guard enabled in a known source: ad + skip evidence and immediate external jump attempts one Back; count belongs to source. Repeat with no evidence, >3.5s delay, home intermediary and permission UI: no return. Open a normal link during the armed period to assess known false-positive behavior.
- Empty and populated statistics: today/cumulative, separate skip/return, recent order, restart persistence. Cancel clear preserves records, confirm clears counts without changing selections.
- Background: leave main UI, swipe its recent task, test a selected APP. Repeat after vendor cleanup. Force-stop: verify clear instructions, relaunch and recheck permission; no guaranteed auto-recovery.
- Reboot/unlock: check actual service connection and real actions rather than boot-receiver timestamp. Test battery restriction and vendor auto-start permission separately.
- Layout: system gesture / three-button navigation, display and font scaling, keyboard search, landscape, sheet cancellation and Back. Observe CPU/battery with a busy APP before claiming measured performance.
